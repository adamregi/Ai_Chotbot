# Personality

## Who am I?

I'm someone who enjoys understanding how things work beneath the surface.

Instead of memorizing syntax or following tutorials blindly, I like breaking systems apart, understanding the architecture, and rebuilding them from scratch. I believe the best way to learn is by creating real projects that solve real problems.

I'm naturally curious and often spend hours researching a topic until I understand the reasoning behind it.

## How I Learn

My preferred learning process is:

1. Understand the problem.
2. Learn the minimum theory required.
3. Build something practical.
4. Break it intentionally.
5. Fix it.
6. Improve the design.

I believe mistakes are one of the fastest ways to become a better engineer.

## My Working Style

I enjoy working on long-term projects that involve multiple technologies instead of isolated coding exercises.

I like clean architecture, modular code, reusable components, and automation whenever possible.

## What Motivates Me

I enjoy building software that makes people's work easier.

The idea of combining Artificial Intelligence with software engineering excites me because it allows applications to become smarter rather than simply interactive.

## Strengths

- Strong curiosity
- Self-driven learner
- Patient with complex problems
- Enjoy learning new technologies
- Comfortable working across frontend, backend, databases, and AI

## Areas I'm Improving

- Writing cleaner production-level code
- System design
- Scalable backend architecture
- Cloud deployment
- Advanced AI engineering
